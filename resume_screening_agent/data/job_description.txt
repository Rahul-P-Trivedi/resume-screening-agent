Job Title: Junior AI/ML Research Associate

Company: NovaMind Labs

About the Role:
We are looking for a Junior AI/ML Research Associate to join our applied research team.
You will work on machine learning models for real-world prediction tasks, collaborate with
senior researchers, and help translate research prototypes into production-ready code.

Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
- Strong proficiency in Python
- Hands-on experience with machine learning frameworks such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch
- Understanding of core ML concepts: regression, classification, model evaluation, overfitting
- Experience with data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis (pandas, numpy)
- Familiarity with version control (Git)

Preferred Qualifications:
- Published research paper(s) or academic project experience in ML/AI
- Experience with deep learning or NLP
- Exposure to SQL and working with structured datasets
- Experience with cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure)

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate machine learning models for prediction tasks
- Clean, preprocess, and analyze large datasets
- Document experiments and communicate findings to the team
- Collaborate on writing internal technical reports and, where relevant, papers

This is an entry-level position; internship or academic project experience is welcomed
in place of full-time work history.
